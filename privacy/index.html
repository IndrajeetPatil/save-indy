Privacy & Legal Notice

Save Indy! is a free, private retro game: what you play stays on your device.

Free and open source

No price, no adverts, no account, and no sign-up. The game is free and open-source software under the CC0 1.0 Universal licence, so anyone can read and inspect its code.

Unofficial fan project & disclaimer

Save Indy! is an unofficial, non-commercial fan project made purely for educational fun and practising JavaScript. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Lucasfilm Ltd., Paramount Pictures, or The Walt Disney Company. Indiana Jones is a trademark of Lucasfilm Ltd.

No money or revenue is made from this game. All pixel artwork and synthesised sound effects are original creations.

Your data stays with you

The game runs entirely inside your browser on this device. There is no backend server that receives your answers, scores, or game progress. Your sound preference (whether audio is muted) is saved in your browser (localStorage) so it stays when you come back. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

Counting visits

Like the rest of indrapatil.com, the site counts visits with Google Analytics: Google learns that the page was opened, with technical details such as your browser and rough location, but never your answers, scores, or interactions. The privacy notice has the details. The game functions identically if your browser or content blocker blocks it.

Get in touch

Spotted a mistake, a wrong question or explanation, or have a privacy concern? Open an issue on GitHub. If you liked this application, explore other applications I've built.
